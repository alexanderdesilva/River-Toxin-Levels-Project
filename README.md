# River-Toxin-Levels-Project
### A Jupyter Notebook analysing river toxic levels of the five most polluted rivers in the UK
From the dataset given by EcoGaurd, we get that from the average level of toxins in the five rivers, Amazon River, Danube River, Mississippi River, Nile River, and Yangtze River, the Yangtze River has the highest concentration of toxins of 10.31mg/l with the Danube River having the lowest concentration of toxins of 5.92mg/l. Moreover, the Yangtze River has the highest concentration of lead, mercury and arsenic but has the lowest concentration of phosphates. We also have a visual for the toxic levels for each river system from 2018 to 2023 which illustrates that the Yangtze River is consistently the most polluted river with only the Nile River on short occasions surpassing such as in 2019. From this data and these visuals, we gather that there needs to be some effort to reduce the toxic levels of the Yangtze River, particularly with the lead and arsenic levels, to increase the welfare of the river to come closer to the cleanliness of those less polluted. River experts can use this to then proceed to find the causes for the toxic levels which will require further research beyond the dataset used to gather these insights.
